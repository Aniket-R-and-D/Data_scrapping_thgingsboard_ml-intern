## 1. Purpose

The Phase R5 reports contain claims that cannot be supported by the preserved artifacts: a
"double-blind human audit" that never happened, a "held-out" evaluation set that overlaps its
training set, a "99%" figure that measures validator acceptance rather than semantic accuracy, an
unsupported "~90–95% semantic correctness" estimate, and "100% safe" statements that overstate
what was actually tested. This notice corrects the record. The original reports are preserved as
historical evidence and are visibly marked, not rewritten.

## 2. Corrected facts (explicit statements)

### 2.1 No human audit occurred
- **Zero (0) human reviewers** have ever scored any output of the explanation layer, in Phase R5
  or in any earlier phase.
- Human review status is **PENDING**, not complete.

### 2.2 Reviewer scores were generated by programmatic heuristics
- **Phase R5 §4 "Formal Double-Blind Human Audit":** the two "specialists" are hardcoded
  `if/else` branches in `src/explain/audit_evaluator.py` (lines 120–143; the source itself
  comments `"Independent review simulation"`, line 103). Reviewer 1's score is a function of
  `is_faithful`/`fallback_used`; Reviewer 2's score is a function of `is_faithful` and whether
  the text contains `"DISPATCH"` or `"Investigate"`. The reported Cohen's Kappa (0.6604)
  reproduces exactly by recomputing it from the stored per-case scores — it is a property of the
  simulation, not of any inter-rater consensus.
- **Phase 6 §4 "Human-Reviewed Sample Audit":** all 30 stored scores take exactly **two**
  value combinations, determined solely by `fallback_used`
  (21 cases: fallback used → factual 4 / actionability 5; 9 cases: no fallback → factual 5 /
  actionability 4). No generator script for these scores exists anywhere in the repository. The
  reported means (4.27, 4.73) are arithmetic consequences of this two-value rule.

### 2.3 The prior "held-out" test set is contaminated
Basis: `training_data/remediated_explanation_train.jsonl` (450 records) vs.
`training_data/remediated_explanation_test_heldout.jsonl` (100 records).

- **Exact input duplication:** **50 of 100 test records have input payloads that are verbatim
  duplicates of training records** (30 distinct payload values). The prior verification report's
  phrasing "30 out of 100 held-out test payloads … identical duplicates" counted *distinct
  values*, not records; the record-level figure is higher.
- **Exact completion duplication:** **63 of 100 test target completions are verbatim duplicates
  of training targets** (42 distinct values).
- **Device overlap:** 43 of 46 unique test devices (93.5%) also appear in training.
- **Incident overlap:** 33 of 44 active-incident test rows (AC = 0 V, `ONLINE_POWER_LOSS`, or
  `CONNECTIVITY_ISSUE` tier) have a training record from the **same device, same device state,
  same risk tier, with identical AC voltage and mains-loss duration within 2.0 h** — i.e.,
  adjacent snapshots of the same ongoing physical event sit on both sides of the split.
  Reconstructing source timestamps from the generator's input pool
  (`remediated_shadow_predictions.jsonl` + `remediated_features_matrix.parquet`), 19 of 27
  distinctive (near-unique) test rows have a same-device, same-state training record within
  24 hours.
- **Structural cause (code evidence):** `src/explain/generate_explanation_dataset.py` sampled
  rows uniformly at random (row-level, seed 42) from one 90-day backtest pool and split
  **positionally** (`dataset_examples[:-100]` vs. the last 100) with no device, incident, or
  temporal grouping. Payloads carry no timestamp, which made this contamination invisible to
  earlier checks.
- Consequently, the test file must not be described as "held-out", "strictly isolated", or
  "completely excluded from fine-tuning". (Its retirement as a development/debugging benchmark
  and the construction of clean evaluation sets are Stage D work, pending gate approval.)

### 2.4 The "99%" result is validator acceptance on a contaminated benchmark — not independently verified semantic accuracy
- Recomputed from the preserved 100-case export
  (`reports/phase_r5_raw_evaluation_100_cases.json`): the remediated model's outputs were
  **accepted by the fixed validator in 99 of 100 cases** (1 fallback; sole rejection Case 24,
  which wrote `9.9%` where the payload probability is `0.0985` — displayed as `9.8%` by the
  canonical rounding — a drift beyond the ±0.05 tolerance).
- This is a measurement of *validator acceptance*, on a benchmark whose inputs overlap training
  (§2.3), against a validator with demonstrated false-acceptance blind spots: the prior
  adversarial suite passed **10 of 10** semantic falsifications (swapped sensor values, wrong
  units, decimal/percentage confusion, negated mains claims, fabricated hardware failures,
  missing-sensor-vs-missing-hardware confusion, stale-data-as-current, wrong branch/bank,
  excessive urgency, missing sections). Concrete example from the same export: legacy Case 2
  (`BOI-DOBSON`) **passed** the fixed validator while claiming an "Acoustic sensor detection
  value is 14.0 V" (no such sensor exists in the payload) and "CRITICAL" risk at probability
  0.0015.
- The remediated model was fine-tuned to reproduce a deterministic template, and the test targets
  are that same template; near-identical completion text is therefore expected by construction
  and is **not** evidence of semantic understanding.
- **No independent verification of semantic accuracy exists for the 99% figure.**

### 2.5 Independent semantic accuracy: NOT MEASURED
- The previous estimates "~40% (legacy)" and "~90–95% (remediated)" independent factual
  accuracy (in `reports/phase_r5_evidence_verification.md` §6.3) were **unsupported** and are
  **removed** (see §5 for the marking applied).
- No instrument — human or programmatic — has measured the true semantic accuracy of any
  explanation-layer output. **Independent semantic accuracy status: NOT MEASURED.**
- **HUMAN REVIEW PENDING.** Blank scoring forms for a genuine human-review pack are Stage E
  work; no scores are to be populated until real reviewers supply them.

### 2.6 "100% safe" claims are replaced by precisely scoped results
- Superseded wordings: "Delivered Faithfulness Rate 100.0% — Zero ungrounded claims reach field
  operations" (R5 §3), "Delivered Template Safety 100.0%" (verification report §6.3), and the
  Phase 6 headline "Final Delivered Faithfulness 100.0%".
- Precisely scoped replacement: **In one specific benchmark run of 100 cases, 100 of 100
  delivered outputs were either raw generations accepted by the validator (99) or replaced by
  the deterministic template (1).** This is an observation about that single run, under a
  validator with the known false-acceptance blind spots listed in §2.4, on a contaminated
  benchmark. It is not a general safety property.
- **An accepted output must not be called "safe" solely because it passed the validator.**
- Related hardcode (documented for Stage B/C): `src/explain/explainer.py` (line 240) writes
  `final_delivered_faithfulness: 1.0` as a **constant** into the benchmark JSON ("Enforced by
  deterministic template fallback"), and `reports/remediated_explanation_benchmark.json`
  contains that hardcoded value. This field is a design assumption, not an empirical
  measurement, and is recorded as such in the claim–evidence ledger.
